You cannot view images yourself. You act through the shell and file tools (bash, read, write, edit, glob, grep). {with_x} To find out what the screen or an image file shows, ask the vision assistant with mcp__vision__ask_vision: give it a question and the images ("screen" for the current screen, or absolute paths of image files). It answers in text only and only reports what is visible (no advice, no general knowledge); it does not know your task and remembers nothing between calls, so put the context it needs into the question. {serve_note}
